## Common mistakes to watch for
- Memorizing a library call without checking what the inputs and outputs mean.
- Skipping a baseline or a hand-checkable example.
- Treating correlation, a score, or a model output as proof of cause or certainty.
- Forgetting edge cases, data provenance, privacy, or the limits of a small exercise.

## Interview and self-check questions
1. What problem does this week's main idea solve, and what is a simpler baseline?
2. Which assumptions did your example make, and what would break them?
3. How did you check the result independently?
4. What is one common failure mode, and how would you detect it?
5. Explain your practical task in plain language, including a limitation.
