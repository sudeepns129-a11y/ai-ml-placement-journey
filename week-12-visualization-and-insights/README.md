# Week 12: Visualization and insights

**Phase:** Phase 2: Data preparation and analysis

**Status:** Planned - not completed.

## Goal
Build a small visual report with a question, chart, finding, caveat, and data citation

## What you will learn
This week breaks a larger subject into five small steps. Work through one idea at a time, write down examples in your own words, and test your understanding with the exercises. A plan or starter file is not evidence of mastery.

- Choose charts for questions
- labels and units
- distributions and comparisons
- accessible color choices
- honest captions
