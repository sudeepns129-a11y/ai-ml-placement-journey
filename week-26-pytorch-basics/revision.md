## Common mistakes to watch for
- Memorizing a library call without checking what the inputs and outputs mean.
- Skipping a baseline or a hand-checkable example.
- Treating correlation, a score, or a model output as proof of cause or certainty.
- Forgetting edge cases, data provenance, privacy, or the limits of a small exercise.
